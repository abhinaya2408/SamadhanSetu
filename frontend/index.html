The path from problem to solution

How SamadhanSetu works

Six stages. AI assists at every one; a person decides at the point that matters.

  1. 1

    Report

    A citizen submits a simple description and an optional photo.

  2. 2

    Understand

    AI turns the description into a clear, simple explanation.

  3. 3

    Connect

    Similar reports in the same city are identified and grouped.

  4. 4

    Review

    An authorised university reviewer evaluates the challenge.

  5. 5

    Build

    Suitable students join a project and develop a solution together.

  6. 6

    Measure

    Evidence and outcomes are documented and reviewed.

Look before you build

Discover solutions that already exist

Before a team builds something new, SamadhanSetu looks through this platform's own solution repository and suggests what might be reused or adapted.

Recommendations are search signals, not verdicts. A mentor verifies suitability, licensing and safety — and the platform never claims to search the wider web.
