The path from problem to solution

Six steps, with a human gate at the decision

AI never approves, rejects, or scores anything on its own. It assists; people decide.

  1. 1

    Citizen submits

    The problem is stored exactly as written — never rewritten by AI.

  2. 2

    AI screens

    Summarises the problem, finds missing details and flags government dependency.

  3. 3

    Human decides

    A faculty or admin reviewer approves, declines, or asks clarifications, with a written reason.

  4. 4

    University forms a team

    Deterministic skill-based matching proposes students; a mentor makes the call.

  5. 5

    Students build & report

    Tasks, milestones, evidence and weekly reports are tracked in the open.

  6. 6

    Verified impact

    Results are recorded with their limitations — claims are never inflated.

What this platform does — and does not — claim

This is a working demonstration. It is careful about what it says it can do.

  • ✓AI assists, humans decide. Screening produces a recommendation; the authoritative record is a human review with a written reason.
  • ✓Deterministic matching. Student matching uses transparent weighted criteria you can inspect — not a black box.
  • ✓Honest search scope. Prior-solution search reads only this deployment's internal repository; it does not search the web.
  • ✓Labelled demo data. Every seeded statistic, score and sponsorship carries a visible Demo data tag.
  • ✓Limitations stated. Impact reports record what was and was not verified, including tests still pending.
